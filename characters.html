Trainers, partners and rivals

Characters

Meet the Protagonists

The heroes of the story are young trainers and their Pokémon partners, who learn that friendship is the strongest power of all.

Protagonist

Ash Ketchum

Ash is a cheerful, determined ten-year-old from Pallet Town who wants to become a Pokémon Master. He often rushes into things without thinking, which gets him into trouble but also shows his big heart. When his first partner won't obey him, he doesn't give up and slowly earns its trust. He cares deeply about every Pokémon he meets, wild or not. His courage and stubborn optimism inspire everyone around him.

Voice actors: Rica Matsumoto (Japanese); Sarah Natochenny (English)

Protagonist

Pikachu

Pikachu is an Electric-type Pokémon who becomes Ash's very first partner. At the start it refuses to listen to him and will not even stay in its Poké Ball. Even so, it bravely steps in to protect Ash when danger comes. Pikachu is stubborn and proud but also loyal, and it shows its strength through powerful electric attacks. The friendship between Ash and Pikachu is the heart of the whole movie.

Voice actors: Ikue Ōtani (Japanese); Ikue Ōtani (English)

Protagonist

Verity

Verity is a friendly trainer who joins Ash on his journey. She is warm, curious and quick to encourage the people around her. She loves Pokémon and tries to understand how each one feels. When the group faces trouble, she stays calm and helps keep everyone together. Her kindness makes her a great travel companion for Ash.

Voice actors: Shiori Sato (Japanese); Suzy Myers (English)

Protagonist

Sorrel

Sorrel is a confident young trainer who ends up traveling with Ash and Verity. He is serious and likes a challenge, so he often pushes himself and others to get better. At first he can seem distant, but he soon shows he cares about his friends. He learns that working as a team can be stronger than going it alone. Sorrel adds balance and skill to the group.

Voice actors: Kanata Hongō (Japanese); David Oliver Nelson (English)

Meet the Antagonists

These characters get in the way of Ash's journey, from a determined rival to a mysterious dark force.

Antagonist

Cross

Cross is a skilled and ambitious trainer who wants the Rainbow Wing for himself. He is a tough rival who is willing to go to great lengths to win. His determination puts him in direct conflict with Ash on Mount Tensei.

Voice actors: Ryōta Ōsaka (Japanese); Billy Bob Thompson (English)

Antagonist

Marshadow

Marshadow is a mysterious Pokémon who appears in the shadows of the story. Its dark power spreads through the mountain and corrupts the Pokémon living there. It becomes the main threat that Ash and his friends must overcome.

Voice actors: Kōichi Yamadera (Japanese); Simona Berman (English)

Antagonist

Team Rocket (Jessie, James and Meowth)

Team Rocket is the scheming trio that is always trying to steal Pikachu. They are more comical than truly dangerous, with silly plans that rarely work. Even so, they keep following Ash and Pikachu wherever the adventure goes.

Voice actors: Megumi Hayashibara, Shinichiro Miki, Inuko Inuyama (Japanese); Michele Knotz, Carter Cathcart (English)
