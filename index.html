Synopsis

Ash Ketchum is a ten-year-old from Pallet Town who dreams of becoming a Pokémon Master. On the day he is meant to receive his first partner, he oversleeps, and the only Pokémon left is a Pikachu who refuses to listen to him.

Their rocky start changes when a flock of wild Pokémon attacks and Pikachu stands up to protect Ash. Soon afterward, the legendary Ho-Oh flies overhead and drops a shining Rainbow Wing. Determined to meet it, Ash sets off on a journey.

Along the way he meets new friends and rivals, faces a mysterious danger on Mount Tensei, and learns what it really means to trust a partner through everything.
